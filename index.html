    Your teacher’s grade is final. This coach reads patterns, not meaning, so a 4 here means your sentences have the right shape and depth. It recognizes its own examples, so the thinking has to be yours.

    The rubric behind the score (AP Lang rhetorical analysis, Row B: Evidence and Commentary)
    1. Restates. Repeats the quote, restates the thesis, or offers nothing relevant.
    2. Summarizes. Evidence is mostly general; commentary summarizes it without explaining how it supports the claim.
    3. Explains some. Specific, relevant evidence; explains how some of it relates to the argument, but the line of reasoning is missing or faulty.
    4. Argues a line of reasoning. Specific evidence supports the claims; explains how at least one rhetorical choice contributes to the writer’s argument, purpose, or message.
    5. Argues with multiple choices. Consistently explains how the evidence supports the reasoning and how more than one rhetorical choice contributes to the writer’s purpose.
